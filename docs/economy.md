# Economy — resources and extraction

## TL;DR
- Two top-level classes: **streaming** (Power) and **stockpile** (everything else).
- Up to 10 resources per map: 4 universal stockpiles + 2 strategic stockpiles + up to 4 environment-locked feedstocks.
- Power is rate-only — no battery cap. SupCom-flavor. HUD shows real-time income minus drain.
- Stockpiles deplete from finite map deposits. Maps run dry; expansion is the answer.
- Resource flow primitives (`give`, `trade`, `share_overflow`) are the seam for enclave karma in `docs/campaign.md`.

## Scope
Owns the resource catalog, the streaming-vs-stockpile model, the extraction model, finite-deposit semantics, and the primitives that resource-sharing systems will use. Does NOT own enclave karma rules (those are `docs/campaign.md`); does NOT own the tech costs that resources gate (those are `docs/tech.md`); does NOT own per-unit / per-structure consumption rates (those are content data inside individual Schematics).

## The two resource classes

**Streaming resources** are *rates*. They have income/sec and drain/sec. No accumulation. If income < drain, things start to fail (build slows, weapons starve, shields drop).

**Stockpile resources** are *quantities*. They accumulate in storage. Construction and tech research consume them as one-time costs.

For the prototype and v1: **Power is the only streaming resource. Everything else is stockpile.**

## The 10-resource model

### Tier 0 — bootstrap (always present)

| Resource | Class | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Power** | streaming | Generated by extractors and reactors. Consumed by everything ongoing. |
| **Scrap** | stockpile | Harvested from debris fields. Cheap, abundant, finite per map. |

### Tier 1 — refined (always present)

| Resource | Class | Refines from |
|---|---|---|
| **Alloy** | stockpile | Scrap + Power (refinery structure) |
| **Fuel** | stockpile | Local feedstock + Power (synthesizer structure) |

### Tier 2 — strategic (always present)

| Resource | Class | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Tech Fragment** | stockpile | Recovered from Ruins (PvE) or salvaged from enemy units (PvP). See `docs/tech.md`. |
| **Exotic Matter** | stockpile | Rare deposits + Meteor Shower drops. See `docs/events.md`. |

### Environment-locked feedstocks (presence varies by map)

These exist only on certain environments and are usually inputs to Fuel synthesis or specialty structures.

| Resource | Class | Where it exists |
|---|---|---|
| **Biomass** | stockpile (renewable) | Cradle only — organic farms; regrows over time |
| **Helium-3** | stockpile | Ashen Eye primarily; rare on gas-giant systems later |
| **Water-Ice** | stockpile | Rustflats, Cryomantle, some asteroids |
| **Rare Earths** | stockpile | Geology-dependent — Rustflats and The Drift especially |

A map declares which environment-locked resources are present via its `environment` Schematic (see `docs/physics.md`). Up to 4 environment-locked feedstocks can be present at once, for a total of 10 resources max.

## Power — the streaming model

- Power has `income/sec` (sum of all generator outputs, environment-modified) and `drain/sec` (sum of all ongoing consumers: radar, jammers, shields, factory production, life support, etc.).
- HUD shows `net = income − drain`. The display is a real-time meter. There is no stockpile.
- When `net < 0`:
  - Construction and research slow proportionally — you build at the rate you can afford.
  - Defensive systems (shields, radar, jammers) brown-out in declared priority order; some pulse on/off.
  - Weapons keep firing from existing charges but cannot recharge.
- Restoring positive net brings everything back to nominal with no recovery lag.

**Why streaming-only.** It's the SupCom signature. It rewards reading the meter, balancing production against consumption, engineering for spikes. It punishes inattention. It is *the* economy loop.

**Tradeoff accepted.** A battery cap would let players store surplus and burst-spend. We're not doing that — it's an extra system, it removes the discipline, and it makes the HUD lie. Streaming-only it is. (Re-evaluate after playtest if the prototype reveals a UX problem.)

## Stockpile model

- Each stockpile resource has `current` and `capacity` (storage limit).
- Capacity is sum of storage structures owned. Default capacity is small — building storage is a real choice.
- When `current >= capacity`, additional extraction **spills**: lost, logged in HUD, and (importantly) **routable** — see resource-flow primitives below.
- Construction consumes stockpile at the moment of placement, not over time. Research consumes at the start of a research project.

---

## Extraction model

- **Extractor structures** are placed on deposit tiles defined by the map.
- Each extractor consumes Power and produces a stockpile resource at a rate.
- Extraction rate is multiplied by:
  - **Deposit richness** (per-tile)
  - **Environment modifier** (e.g., Biomass extractors are 0.4× in Cradle Winter)
  - **Power supply ratio** (under-powered extractors slow proportionally)
- **Deposits are finite.** Each deposit tile has a quantity. When exhausted, the extractor still draws Power but produces nothing. The HUD warns the player; the extractor can be decommissioned for partial Scrap refund.
- Some deposits are **renewable** (Biomass) — they regrow over real time, capped at the deposit's max.

This means *maps run dry*, and players must expand. Deliberate pressure-toward-conflict.

## Resource flow primitives

Stockpile resources can be **transferred** between actors (player, ally, NPC enclave). These primitives are the seam the enclave karma system reads from.

| Primitive | Behavior |
|---|---|
| `give(amount, resource, to)` | One-way gift. Subtracts from sender, adds to recipient. Recipient cannot refuse. |
| `trade(give_bundle, want_bundle, to)` | Negotiated exchange. Recipient accepts or rejects based on its priorities. |
| `share_overflow(to)` | Standing instruction: when this player would spill at full capacity, redirect the overflow to the named recipient instead of losing it. |

These are implemented in the engine; the karma *rules* — what counts as generous, what counts as hoarding, how karma decays — are Schematic data in `docs/campaign.md`. Moddable.

**Power is NOT directly transferable** (streaming, no stockpile). But Power *generators* can be gifted (transfer ownership of a structure), which effectively gives the recipient the ongoing income. This becomes a meaningful diplomatic primitive.

## Visualizing the depth on mobile

Locked decision: **accept complexity for depth.** The HUD is designed to handle 10 resources without trimming them.

Sketch (detailed in `docs/ui-ux.md`):
- **Always visible:** Power net rate, single most-stressed stockpile (auto-selected by the engine — whichever resource is currently bottlenecking production), tech-fragment count.
- **One swipe away:** Full stockpile bar (compact, all visible at once).
- **One tap to expand:** Per-resource detail with income rate, drain rate, capacity, projected time-to-cap or time-to-empty.

The principle: most of the time, the player only needs Power + one other number. The HUD surfaces *that* number contextually rather than asking the player to scan.

## Open questions

- OPEN[2026-05-27]: Renewability beyond Biomass — should any other env-locked feedstock regrow (e.g., Water-Ice on a tidal moon, Helium-3 cosmic-ray accumulation)? Lean Biomass-only — keeps the renewable mechanic distinctive.
- OPEN[2026-05-27]: Tech Fragment and Exotic Matter tradeability — does enclave karma extend to strategic resources, or only to the bulk economy? Lean tradeable; it makes the moral choices harder. Resolve in `docs/campaign.md`.
- OPEN[2026-05-27]: Power overflow — vents harmlessly today. Should it instead charge a *small* (~5s of average drain) short-term buffer for spike absorption? Lean no; pure rate is the signature. Re-evaluate after playtest.
- OPEN[2026-05-27]: Spill-vs-share UX — when a player has `share_overflow` set to multiple enclaves, what's the routing policy? Round-robin? Highest-karma-first? Resolve in `docs/campaign.md` and `docs/ui-ux.md`.
